Hackculture was a new startup working on a platform to organize and manage hackathons at scale, enabling seamless participation, project submissions, and community engagement. They are aiming to partner up with world class enterprise companies to manage and conduct their hackathon projects.


They have requested to build a consistent and professional identity ahead of launch. My contributions included designing a new brand identity, logo redesign along with product design aspects, beginning with their landing page.

Primary Challenges

Through comprehensive user research and analytics analysis, I identified 4 critical problem areas affecting the shopping experience.

Fragmented Branding

Hackculture’s inconsistent identity and weak visuals reduced recognition and differentiation.

Unpolished User Experience

Disorganized layouts and weak navigation hindered both organizers and participants.

Limited Trust & Credibility

Lack of solid branding and UI reduced trust with companies and sponsors.

Scalability Challenges

No solid guidelines or systems led to inconsistency as the platform grew.

After extensive UX research and launch strategy, following are all the UI/UX Designs I have made for the client before the launch.

DESIGN SOLUTIONS

  • Responsive landing page for students, professionals.
  • Enterprise partner page to drive business.
  • White-label hackathon info webpage template.
  • Admin user database management system.
  • Admin hackathon applications management system.
  • User portal for application tracking.
  • Pitch deck for partners and investors.
  • Promotional materials for early hackathons.

The redesigned e-commerce platform has achieved significant improvements in user engagement, conversion rates, and overall business metrics.

80K+ users, 30+ hackathons within 1st year
Strong identity boosted trust with users and partners.
Partnered with gaints like AWS, Google, others.
My designs shaped the success of the launch.
