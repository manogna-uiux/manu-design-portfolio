IncubateHub is a B2B consulting company that conducted a hiring hackathon for a Finance Tracker mobie application for UI/UX Designers.


The problem statement was to design an app that lets users manage their finances, track their expenses, payments and also educate them on concepts of investments and tax saving.

Primary Challenges

Through extensive user research and market analysis, I identified 4 critical problem areas affecting user adoption and engagement.

Manual Tracking Hassles

Recording income and expenses is time-consuming, and without automation users lose consistency.

Missing Planning Tools

Without budgeting or forecasting, users overspend and struggle financially.

Guidance Overload

Endless, conflicting online advice leaves users overwhelmed, confused, and unsure whom to trust.

Shared Expenses Tracking

Sharing expenses with others is complicated, often causing confusion and missed contributions.

The design strategy focused on creating a simple, educational, and trustworthy mobile experience that would guide users through their financial journey with confidence.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

  • Smart expense tracking via bank linking.
  • Simple budgeting with goals and forecasts.
  • Shared expenses with splits and reminders.
  • Spending stats to reveal habits, improvements.
  • Custom alerts for bills and budgets.
  • Clean, modern interface for easy navigation.
  • Personalized coaching with AI recommendations.
  • Educational hub with tips on investing.

The design demonstrated strong skills in problem-solving, user-centered design, and won 1st place in the hackathon contest.
