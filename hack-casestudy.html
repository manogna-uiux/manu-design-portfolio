02 The Solution

The existing e-commerce platform was struggling with low conversion rates, high cart abandonment, and poor user engagement. The interface was outdated, navigation was confusing, and the mobile experience was subpar.


Key challenges included poor product discovery, complicated checkout process, and lack of personalization features.

Primary Challenges

Through comprehensive user research and analytics analysis, I identified 4 critical problem areas affecting the shopping experience.

Poor Product Discovery

Users struggled to find relevant products due to ineffective search and filtering systems.

Complex Checkout

The checkout process was too long with unnecessary steps, leading to high abandonment rates.

Lack of Personalization

Users received generic recommendations that didn't match their preferences or shopping history.

Mobile Experience

The mobile interface was not optimized for touch interactions and had poor performance.

Our design strategy focused on creating a modern, intuitive e-commerce experience that would guide users seamlessly from product discovery to purchase completion.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

  • Advanced search with AI-powered recommendations
  • Streamlined checkout with guest and social login options
  • Personalized product recommendations and wishlists
  • Mobile-first responsive design with touch optimization
  • Real-time inventory and shipping information
  • Social proof and customer reviews integration
  • Multiple payment options and secure checkout
  • Post-purchase engagement and loyalty programs

The redesigned e-commerce platform has achieved significant improvements in user engagement, conversion rates, and overall business metrics.

45% Conversion Increase
60% Cart Abandonment Reduction
80% Mobile Revenue Growth
35% Average Order Value
